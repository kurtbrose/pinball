# Conventional lower playfield

Reference: the user's overhead Star Trek photo and perspective Pinball Star screenshot, both oriented with flippers at the bottom. Both show triangular slings framing the center fan, protected inlanes feeding the outer flipper ends and separate outer loss lanes. We borrow that lower-field structure, not machine measurements, artwork, upper shots or physical calibration.

Baseline geometry is preserved in `layout-before-conventional-lower.json`. Current tuning is in `firstTable`. Lower components are paired around x=2.725, the center of the playfield between x=0.35 and the x=5.1 shooter divider. Flipper pivots are x=1.4/4.05 at y=8.2; lane dividers are x=0.9/4.55. Triangular slings extend to y=7.4 and have explicit inward-active normals. Their inlane-facing walls are passive. Prototype calibration is unchanged.

- Evidence: `node scripts/check.js` runs both regressions and generated evaluation; `node scripts/evaluate-layout.js` runs the latter alone. The current evaluation executes 2,133 deterministic simulations from 217 clear spatial starts, 36 wall arrivals and 12 bumper approaches. It reports zero required failures and 12 diagnostics, preserving their reproducible configurations and trajectories in `results/layout-evaluation.json`. Timeouts are censored; low-speed states responsive to input are not mislabeled as proven traps.
- Negative tests include a removed-collector variant discovered by the automatic feed matrix, an inaccessible passive box found by generated spatial probes, and a rejected collector/post pocket from the earlier review. These verify finding behavior, not just current coordinates. Tests distinguish passive sling contacts from inward firing.
- Browser startup, launch, left/right paddles, nudge and reset verified in Edge; screenshot inspected for conventional lower-field proportions and correctly highlighted inward sling faces. Passing these checks is not evidence of good feel.
- Decision: prefer the supplied conventional arrangement; keep the numerical evaluator bounded and use it as a guard against broken routes. No automatic player, optimization engine, new modes or editor introduced. Human playtesting and broader second-action timing coverage remain outstanding.
