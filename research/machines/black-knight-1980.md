# Black Knight — Williams, 1980

- **Model:** original Black Knight; not Black Knight 2000 or Sword of Rage.
- **Source/evidence:** [Ray Johnson rulesheet](https://www.pinball.org/rules/blackknight.html), enhanced revision of Larry DeMar's original. Firsthand/designer-derived rules; confidence high for listed interactions, exact timing adjustable.
- **Observed:** two playfield levels; timed drop banks qualify player-operated Magna-Save; magnets attempt to recover a threatened ball into a return lane. Upper storage starts two/three-ball multiball. Inlanes can light timed ramp/spinner awards.
- **Cards:** [drop bank and qualify/collect](../mechanisms/targets-and-progress.md), [Magna-Save](../mechanisms/control-and-feedback.md#magna-save), [physical lock](../mechanisms/capture-and-routing.md#physical-lock-and-staged-release).
- **Proposed lesson:** pair rescue with earned agency; review magnetic release into a controllable feed. An ordinary target can borrow qualification without copying the magnet or upper level.
- **Gaps:** no measured geometry or photographed feed analysis; no inventor/first-use claim. Current engine lacks magnets, locks and simultaneous multiball.
