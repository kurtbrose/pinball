# Theatre of Magic — Bally, 1995

- **Source/evidence:** [Bowen Kerins rulesheet](https://www.pinball.org/rules/theatreofmagic.html), A1.0/P-1 context with later updates. Firsthand route descriptions; confidence high descriptively, timing/revision dependent.
- **Observed:** ordinary lower flipper pair and inlanes; staircase ramps end at inlanes. Inner loops connect through the trunk region; the right loop can return down the center. The trunk rotates among four faces. A magnetic Spirit Ring transfers a ball between elevated returns. Top rollovers allow lane change.
- **Cards:** [control base](../mechanisms/control-and-feedback.md), [loops/ramps](../mechanisms/routes-and-feeds.md), [rotating toy](../mechanisms/signature-devices.md#rotating-multi-face-toy), [rollovers](../mechanisms/targets-and-progress.md#rollover-lanes-and-lane-change).
- **Proposed lesson:** review strong and weak routes separately; a successful traveling shot can still return dangerously. Keep the ring as a machine-specific magnetic-transfer variant, not a new generic ramp family.
- **Gaps:** no geometric measurements or feed distributions. Ring transfer requires elevation and magnetic/capture behavior; this seed has no dedicated implementation model for it.
