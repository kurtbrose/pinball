# FunHouse — Williams, 1990

- **Source/evidence:** [Mark Phaedrus rulesheet, 16 Apr 1991](https://www.pinball.org/rules/funhouse.html). Firsthand rules with manufacturer corrections acknowledged; confidence high for named interactions, exact rules revision dependent.
- **Observed:** controlled plunge can stop in a top loop and roll into Rudy's Hideout. The center ramp normally feeds the left inlane, or the Steps plunger when enabled. Rudy's mouth becomes a multiball entry at midnight. Ordered ramp/Gangway/loop shots award combos.
- **Cards:** [plunge/skill shot](../mechanisms/control-and-feedback.md#manual-plunge-and-skill-shot), [diverter](../mechanisms/capture-and-routing.md#state-dependent-diverter), [ramp](../mechanisms/routes-and-feeds.md#ramp-to-inlane), [combo](../mechanisms/targets-and-progress.md#timed-shot-combo).
- **Proposed lesson:** reuse an entrance for a visible alternate destination; ensure both destinations have useful downstream play. A simple single-plane timed follow-up can borrow the combo concept.
- **Gaps:** no exact coordinates, actuator timing or return-speed evidence; mouth/capture and Steps routing need extensions.
