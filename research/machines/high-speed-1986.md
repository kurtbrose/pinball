# High Speed — Williams, 1986

- **Model:** High Speed; not The Getaway: High Speed II.
- **Source/evidence:** [Bill Ung v1.10 rulesheet](https://www.pinball.org/rules/highspeed.html), including machine instruction-card transcription. Confidence high for broad routes; adjustments affect qualification.
- **Observed:** perimeter Freeways with spinners; lower inlanes light the opposite Freeway. An eject hole feeds the upper-right flipper. Ramp destinations change with chase/lock state; side hideouts store balls. Targets/inlanes can relight kickback.
- **Cards:** [orbit](../mechanisms/routes-and-feeds.md#perimeter-orbit), [spinner and inlane-lit shot](../mechanisms/targets-and-progress.md), [upper feed and kickback](../mechanisms/control-and-feedback.md), [capture/lock/diverter](../mechanisms/capture-and-routing.md).
- **Proposed lesson:** connect a return to a natural follow-up instead of distributing isolated awards. Prototype a same-plane traveling route before adding ramp/lock states.
- **Gaps:** no speed distributions, scale or measured aim windows. State-dependent ramps and kickout mechanics require extensions.
