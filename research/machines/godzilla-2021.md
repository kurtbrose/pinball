# Godzilla — Stern, 2021

- **Model:** original Pro/Premium/LE distinctions; do not infer Premium features on Pro or treat later anniversary editions as identical evidence.
- **Source/evidence:** [manufacturer description](https://www.sternpinball.com/game/Godzilla/) and [2021 feature matrix](https://sternpinball.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/Godzilla-Pinball-Feature-Matrix-3kjhasdf.pdf). Primary documented; confidence high for device/model presence, no claim about feel.
- **Observed:** all models list three flippers, spinners, standups, a pop bumper and wireforms. Premium/LE add a moving skyscraper, collapsing bridge and rotating target bank exposing a spinner/jump ramp. Magna Grab catches from several paths and can redirect toward an upper flipper.
- **Cards:** [upper feed](../mechanisms/control-and-feedback.md#upper-flipper-feed), [spinner/standup](../mechanisms/targets-and-progress.md), [wireform](../mechanisms/routes-and-feeds.md#wireform-return), [moving route toy](../mechanisms/signature-devices.md#moving-ramp-or-target-facade).
- **Proposed lesson:** make a centerpiece alter route choices rather than just decorate. Treat the magnetic catch as a routing variant, distinct from player-operated Magna-Save.
- **Gaps:** no measured transitions or timing; jump trajectories, magnets, moving elevated geometry and locks require extensions.
