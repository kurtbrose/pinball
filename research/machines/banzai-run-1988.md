# Banzai Run — Williams, 1988

- **Source/evidence:** [Bowen Kerins rulesheet](https://www.pinball.org/rules/banzairun.html). Firsthand recollection; author explicitly warns about accuracy. Confidence medium for broad device presence, provisional for timing and specific feed outcomes.
- **Observed:** a magnetic lift transfers the ball to the vertical backglass Hill. Hill losses return to the main table through a ramp. Captive balls and secondary flippers provide separate objectives. A lower outlane has a kickback.
- **Cards:** [vertical field](../mechanisms/signature-devices.md#vertical-secondary-playfield), [captive ball](../mechanisms/targets-and-progress.md#captive-ball), [upper feed/kickback](../mechanisms/control-and-feedback.md).
- **Proposed lesson:** a secondary area needs a complete entry/control/return contract. Borrowing a brief change of pace need not entail a vertical playfield.
- **Gaps:** corroborate lift and return details with an original manual before implementation. No physical dimensions or force data; regional dynamics and transfer are unsupported.
