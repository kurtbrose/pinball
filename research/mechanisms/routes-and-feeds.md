# Routes and feeds

Placement, tradeoffs and probes are our engineering guidance; source records establish descriptive examples only. None of these cards supplies measured coordinates.

## Perimeter orbit

- **ID/category:** perimeter-orbit / traveling shot.
- **Behavior/use:** an outer route carries a strong shot around the upper field and back down another side; supports flow.
- **Placement/tradeoff:** define both exits and a weak-shot fallback. A fast return may require a timely control action.
- **Evidence:** [High Speed](../machines/high-speed-1986.md), outer Freeways; [Theatre of Magic](../machines/theatre-of-magic-1995.md), right orbit.
- **Status:** Geometry candidate for a single-plane version; switches/diverters Extension.
- **Probe:** full route from a flipper, underspeed rollback, late exit and both-side control opportunities.

## Inner loop

- **ID/category:** inner-loop / traveling shot.
- **Behavior/use:** a shorter turnaround supplies another aim and a different exit rhythm.
- **Placement/tradeoff:** entrance selection must remain distinct from adjacent targets; watch straight-center returns.
- **Evidence:** [Theatre of Magic](../machines/theatre-of-magic-1995.md), paired inner-loop entrances.
- **Status:** Geometry candidate; route-specific scoring Extension.
- **Probe:** both directions, partial completion, grazing entrance and returned ball near the center drain.

## Ramp to inlane

- **ID/category:** ramp-inlane / elevated return.
- **Behavior/use:** an uphill shot earns a separated return to a chosen flipper.
- **Placement/tradeoff:** clear entrance, success threshold and failed ascent; crossing another shot requires collision separation.
- **Evidence:** [Theatre of Magic](../machines/theatre-of-magic-1995.md), staircase ramps.
- **Status:** Extension: elevation or an explicit deterministic route layer with entry/exit state; no existing ramp physics.
- **Probe:** minimum successful speed, rollback, rail departure and terminal feed control.

## Wireform return

- **ID/category:** wireform-return / elevated routing.
- **Behavior/use:** a constrained elevated channel delivers a completed shot over other geometry.
- **Placement/tradeoff:** terminal drop height/direction matter more than cosmetic rails; avoid concealed uncontrolled feeds.
- **Evidence:** [Godzilla](../machines/godzilla-2021.md) manufacturer describes wireforms.
- **Status:** Extension: separated collision path and reproducible transition; a painted curve is insufficient.
- **Probe:** slow transit, terminal drop, concurrent lower-route ball and stalled-route release if multiball is introduced.
