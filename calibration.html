Flipper calibration

Same ball, geometry and surface damping. Compare the current servo with experimental stroke / hold actuation.

Launch cases start just above the resting left flipper at 25%, 50%, 75% or 90% of its length. Two additional mid-flipper cases introduce the ball halfway through the stroke or after the flipper is fully raised. Incoming shots approach normal to the rubber at 3 m/s. The other flipper and walls are removed.

Angle is measured from right toward up. Rise is measured after separation until the apex or next contact; units use the current oversized prototype scale.

Peak contact speed is sampled after collision solving across the initial contact sequence; exit velocity is sampled after at least 16 ticks and three ticks without an impact. These are fixed-tick diagnostics, not real-machine measurements. Only ball/flipper contacts contribute. These are controlled actuator-state probes, not complete inlane approaches. No result means the ball did not separate during the five-second trial.
